People — privacy

People is a personal contacts app used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

It reads and writes the owner's own notes in the owner's Google Drive, adds reminders to the owner's Google Calendar and Tasks, and updates the owner's Google Contacts. Data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's devices. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any other service.

Contact: the owner, through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
